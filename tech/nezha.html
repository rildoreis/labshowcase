Nezha Inventor's Kit for micro:bit

ELECFREAKS building-block kit with motors and sensors — medium difficulty

Type of techEducational robotics
UseTeaching & Research
Quantity3 kits

What is it?

A construction kit that combines a micro:bit with plastic building blocks, servo motors and sensors. Students build mechanical models — vehicles, cranes, a catapult, a smart lamp — and then program their behaviour in MakeCode. It sits one step above the Cutebot: more building, more parts, more room for open-ended projects.

What's in the kit

  • Nezha expansion board with motor and servo ports and a rechargeable battery
  • Building blocks (LEGO-compatible), motors, servos and sensors
  • micro:bit as the controller; programmed in MakeCode

Ideas for teaching and research

Teaching ideas

  • Project-based units: each group builds and programs a different model and presents it to the class.
  • Technical communication: students write, test and revise assembly instructions for others.
  • Engineering design cycle: plan, build, test, improve — with the robot as the evidence.
  • Cross-curricular STEAM: simple machines, levers, gears and sensors in one build.

Research ideas

  • Maker education: how open-ended building affects engagement and persistence.
  • Multimodal interaction analysis: gesture, talk and handling of parts during group work.
  • Instructional design: comparing printed, video and peer-written build instructions.

Good to know

  • Medium difficulty — plan a first session around one guided build.
  • The expansion board has a rechargeable battery; charge it before a session.
  • Keep the parts sorted in the case compartments to make clean-up quick.
