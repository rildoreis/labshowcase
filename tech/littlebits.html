What is it?

littleBits are small electronic modules — power, sensors, motors, lights, buzzers — that snap together with magnets, so students can build working circuits and inventions in minutes without soldering or wiring. The Gizmos & Gadgets Kit focuses on mechanical inventions (materials for 12 projects with 15 bits); the Code Kit adds a programmable “codeBit” and over 100 classroom activities with 10 lessons for grades 3 and up.

What's in the kit

  • Gizmos & Gadgets Kit: 15 bits plus wheels, gears, servo, motors and craft materials for 12 inventions
  • Code Kit: codeBit, sensors, LED matrix, buttons; 1–3 students per kit; grades 3+
  • Software: the littleBits Code Kit app (block-based, browser or desktop)

Used so far

Nobody in the lab has tried this one yet. If you know it, come and show us — a 20-minute walkthrough would be the first entry here.

Ideas for teaching and research

Teaching ideas

  • Invention challenges: “build something that helps…” prompts, then a pitch in the target language.
  • Vocabulary of cause and effect: if-then language mapped onto input–output circuits.
  • Games and quizzes: the Code Kit's built-in game projects (buzzers, scoreboards) for classroom activities.
  • Low-floor prototyping: fast mock-ups of interactive objects for design thinking sessions.

Research ideas

  • Tinkering and creativity: observing idea generation with modular, no-wiring electronics.
  • Younger learners: early STEM and language integration in grades 3–6.

Good to know
