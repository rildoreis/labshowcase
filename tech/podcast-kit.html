Portable Podcast Kit

Movo MDX-2 bundle — two-person recording setup for computers, tablets and phones

Type of techAudio production
UseTeaching & Research
Quantity1 kit
StatusReady to demo (recording app pending)

What is it?

A compact two-channel recording setup that lets two speakers record clean, separate audio tracks on a laptop, tablet or phone — no studio required. The MDX-2 interface runs on batteries or USB power, so the kit can be carried to a classroom, an office or the field and set up in minutes. It suits podcasting, interviews, pronunciation work and any project where students produce audio rather than only consume it.

What's in the kit

  • 2 dynamic microphones (XLR, with windscreens) on tabletop stands
  • MDX-2 audio interface — 2 inputs, headphone monitoring, up to 24-bit/48 kHz; runs on USB power or 2 AA batteries
  • 2 pairs of headphones for live monitoring
  • Cables and case — 2 XLR, USB-A, USB-C and Lightning
  • Not included: a recording device (any laptop, tablet or phone with a recording app such as Audacity or GarageBand)

Ideas for teaching and research

Teaching ideas

  • Student podcasts: episodic projects where learners plan, script, record and edit a show in the target language.
  • Interview tasks: pair activities with guest speakers, peers or community members, followed by transcription and discussion.
  • Pronunciation and fluency: recorded speaking samples that students compare over the semester.
  • Audio description and narration: voice tracks for accessible media, museum guides or visual novels.
  • Radio drama and storytelling: two-voice readings, dialogues and sound-rich narratives.

Research ideas

  • Spoken-corpus building: clean two-track recordings for discourse, pragmatics or interaction analysis.
  • Oral-history and ethnographic interviews: portable, battery-powered fieldwork setup.
  • Think-aloud and usability studies: capturing participant commentary during task-based sessions.
  • Audio-description reception studies: producing stimuli with consistent recording quality.
  • Learner-speech studies: longitudinal samples of fluency, prosody or accuracy.

Good to know

  • The recording app still needs to be installed on the lab device before the first demo.
  • New equipment — no one has reported experience with it yet, so the first session should be a guided walkthrough.
  • Each microphone records to its own track; keep headphones on while recording and set gain so speech peaks around –12 dB.
