Ray-Ban Meta Smart Glasses

Wayfarer (Gen 2) — camera, open-ear audio and a voice assistant in a pair of glasses

Type of techWearable & AI
UseTeaching & Research
Quantity4 units
StatusSetup pending (being set up)

What is it?

Ordinary-looking glasses with a built-in camera, microphones and small speakers in the arms. The wearer can take first-person photos and video, listen to audio, take calls, and talk to the Meta AI assistant — asking what it sees, translating speech or text, or answering questions hands-free. They pair with a phone through the Meta AI app.

What's in the kit

  • 4 pairs of Ray-Ban Meta Wayfarer glasses (Gen 2)
  • Charging cases and cables
  • Software: Meta AI app on a paired phone

Ideas for teaching and research

Teaching ideas

  • First-person video tasks: students record a tour, a recipe or a how-to while narrating in the target language.
  • Live translation practice: testing and critiquing the assistant's translations in real situations.
  • Describe what you see: oral description tasks captured from the speaker's point of view — a natural fit for audio description training.
  • Fieldwork and interviews: hands-free recording of community visits (with consent).

Research ideas

  • Human–AI interaction in language use: how learners rely on or correct AI translation and description.
  • Egocentric video for interaction analysis: gaze-aligned recordings of classroom or service encounters.
  • Privacy and ethics studies: attitudes toward recording in public and in classrooms.

Good to know

  • Someone is currently working on the setup; the glasses need pairing with a phone and a Meta account.
  • Recording others requires consent; the LED indicator must stay visible and uncovered.
  • Plan for a prescription-free use or clip-on lenses; the frames are standard Wayfarer size.
