# How to use this document

Each section below is one Google Form. Create the form at forms.google.com, copy the questions in order, then embed it on the site: **Send → < > (Embed HTML) → copy the `<iframe>`** and paste it into the matching placeholder in the page (the HTML comment above each placeholder says which form goes where).

Recommended settings for all forms: *Collect email addresses → Verified* (institutional accounts) or *Responder input*; *Limit to 1 response* off; *Show link to submit another response* off; confirmation message as given in each section. Link each form to a Google Sheet (Responses → Link to Sheets) so the team sees sign-ups in one place.

# Form 1 — "I'm interested" (one form, embedded on every technology page)

**Purpose:** a thirty-second sign-up so you know who wants to try which technology.
**Where:** the "I'm interested" box on each technology page. Use one form for all technologies; create a **pre-filled link** for each page (⋮ → *Get pre-filled link*, choose the technology, copy the link, and use that link's URL inside the embed code) so the Technology question is already answered.

**Title:** I'm interested — What's in the Lab
**Description:** Tell us which technology you would like to try and we will get in touch to set up a short demo. It takes about thirty seconds.

1. **Technology** — Dropdown, required
   Portable Podcast Kit · micro:bit Smart Cutebot · Nezha Inventor's Kit · littleBits Kits · Sphero BOLT Power Pack · Omni One · Ray-Ban Meta Smart Glasses · Meta Quest 3 · Cricut Maker 3 · 3D Printers · RoboSapien · Not sure yet — show me a few
2. **Your name** — Short answer, required
3. **Email** — Short answer, required (or collected automatically)
4. **I am a…** — Multiple choice, required
   Faculty / instructor · Graduate student · Undergraduate student · Staff · Visiting researcher · Other
5. **Department or program** — Short answer, optional
6. **What would you like to do with it?** — Checkboxes, required
   Just see it working · Try it myself · Use it in a class · Use it in a research project · Borrow it · Attend a training session
7. **Have you used something like this before?** — Multiple choice, optional
   Never · A little · Comfortable · I could help others
8. **When would suit you?** — Checkboxes, optional
   This week · Next two weeks · This month · No rush
9. **Anything else we should know?** — Paragraph, optional

**Confirmation message:** Thanks! We will write to you within a few days to set up a time. In the meantime, feel free to post ideas on the wall on the technology's page.

# Form 2 — "Propose a project or activity" (Collaborate page)

**Purpose:** capture concrete ideas for lessons, assignments, theses or studies that could use the lab.
**Where:** the "Propose a project or activity" box on the Collaborate page.

**Title:** Propose a project or activity — What's in the Lab
**Description:** A few lines are enough. We read every proposal and reply within a week with next steps: a demo, a pilot session or a planning meeting.

1. **Your name** — Short answer, required
2. **Email** — Short answer, required
3. **Role** — Multiple choice, required
   Faculty / instructor · Graduate student · Undergraduate student · Staff · Visiting researcher · Other
4. **Department or program** — Short answer, optional
5. **Which technologies would it use?** — Checkboxes, required
   (same list as Form 1, plus "Something not in the lab yet")
6. **Working title for the idea** — Short answer, required
7. **What is the idea?** — Paragraph, required
   *Help text:* Course or project, what students or participants would do, and what you hope they would learn or what you want to find out.
8. **This is mainly for…** — Multiple choice, required
   Teaching · Research · Both · Outreach / community
9. **Who would take part?** — Short answer, optional
   *Help text:* e.g., one section of Spanish 2, 20 students; 12 interview participants; a teacher-training workshop.
10. **When?** — Multiple choice, required
    This semester · Next semester · Summer · Not decided
11. **What would you need from the lab?** — Checkboxes, required
    Equipment access · Training for me · Training for my students · Help designing the activity · Help with a research protocol or ethics application · Space for sessions · Someone present at the first session · Not sure yet
12. **Anything else?** — Paragraph, optional

**Confirmation message:** Thank you — your idea is in. We will reply within a week. If it is time-sensitive, email the coordinator directly.

# Form 3 — "Book a demo" (Collaborate page, if you do not use a Calendar appointment schedule)

**Purpose:** a simple booking request when Google Calendar appointment schedules are not available.
**Where:** the "Book a demo" box on the Collaborate page.

**Title:** Book a demo — What's in the Lab
**Description:** Demos take about 30 minutes. Tell us which technologies you want to see and when; we will confirm by email.

1. **Your name** — Short answer, required
2. **Email** — Short answer, required
3. **Technologies to see** — Checkboxes, required (same list as Form 1)
4. **How many people will come?** — Multiple choice, required
   Just me · 2–4 · 5–10 · A whole class (tell us the size below)
5. **Preferred days** — Checkboxes, required
   Monday · Tuesday · Wednesday · Thursday · Friday
6. **Preferred time** — Multiple choice, required
   Morning (9–12) · Early afternoon (12–3) · Late afternoon (3–5)
7. **Specific dates, if any** — Short answer, optional
8. **What do you want to get out of the demo?** — Paragraph, optional

**Confirmation message:** Thanks — we will confirm a slot by email within two working days.

# Form 4 — "Quick poll" (Collaborate page; change the question each semester)

**Purpose:** one-click participation that also tells you what people want.
**Where:** the "What should we add next?" box on the Collaborate page. After a few responses, add the results link (Responses → ⋮ → *Publish results*… or share a screenshot) next to the embed.

**Title:** Quick poll — What's in the Lab
**Description:** One question. Vote and see where the lab goes next.

Choose **one** of these single-question versions and rotate them:

- **Version A — What should we add next?** — Multiple choice
  A 360° camera · A green screen and lighting kit · Tablets with styluses · A second podcast kit · An AI voice/translation workstation · A laser cutter · Other (short answer)
- **Version B — Which training would you attend?** — Multiple choice
  VR for language teaching (Quest 3) · Podcasting with students · micro:bit robots in the classroom · 3D printing basics · Cricut for classroom materials · Smart glasses: recording and ethics
- **Version C — How do you prefer to learn a new tool?** — Multiple choice
  A 30-minute live demo · A short video I can watch anytime · A written step-by-step guide · Trying it with a colleague · A workshop with a lesson plan

Optional second question for any version: **Your email, if you want to hear the result** — Short answer, optional.

**Confirmation message:** Thanks for voting! Results are shared on this page once a month.

# Optional — per-technology quizzes (if you want a quiz block)

If you add a "Test yourself" box to a technology page, a Google Form in **Quiz mode** (Settings → Make this a quiz) works without any code. Three questions is enough. Two examples:

**Meta Quest 3 — three questions**

1. The Quest 3 needs a PC to run. — True / **False**
2. "Mixed reality" on the Quest 3 means… — The headset projects virtual objects into a view of the real room *(correct)* · The user wears two headsets · The headset works only outdoors
3. A good first session length for new users is… — 5 minutes · **15–20 minutes** · 90 minutes

**Portable Podcast Kit — three questions**

1. How many speakers can record at once with the kit? — 1 · **2** · 4
2. The MDX-2 interface can run on… — Mains power only · **USB power or AA batteries** · Solar
3. What is NOT in the kit? — Headphones · Microphones · **A laptop or phone to record on**
